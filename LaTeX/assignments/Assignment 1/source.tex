\section{Equations}
The time-dependent Schr\"odinger equation \citep{schrodinger1926} is given in
Equation~\eqref{eq:schrodinger}:
\begin{equation}
  i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\Psi(\mathbf{r},t) =
  \left[ -\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2\mu}\nabla^{2} + V(\mathbf{r},t) \right]\Psi(\mathbf{r},t)
  \label{eq:schrodinger}
\end{equation}
As discussed by \citet{schrodinger1926}, Equation~\eqref{eq:schrodinger} describes how
the quantum state $\Psi$ of a particle of reduced mass $\mu$ evolves in a potential~$V$.
